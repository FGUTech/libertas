# Citizen Lab Researcher Details State Surveillance Abuse Across Latin America

**TL;DR:** Citizen Lab's Luis Fernando García presented on government abuse of spyware, mass surveillance, and advertising intelligence targeting civil society across Latin America.

## Key Points

- Luis Fernando García (Citizen Lab senior researcher) spoke at regional panel on state surveillance
- Event hosted by CELS, ODIA, Democracia en Red, and Vía Libre civil rights organizations
- Focus on government abuse of spyware technologies against civil society
- Discussion covered mass surveillance programs deployed by Latin American governments
- Addressed use of advertising intelligence for state surveillance purposes
- Panel examined intersection of surveillance tech with democratic oversight and civil space protection
- Represents coordinated regional response to increasing surveillance threats
