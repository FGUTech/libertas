# EFF Names Nicole Ozer as New Executive Director

**TL;DR:** Electronic Frontier Foundation announces Nicole Ozer as new Executive Director, bringing two decades of privacy legislation and surveillance oversight experience including authoring California's warrant requirement for electronic data access.

## Key Points

- Nicole Ozer becomes EFF's new Executive Director, succeeding Cindy Cohn
- Previously served as founding director of ACLU Northern California's Technology and Civil Liberties Program (2004-2025)
- Spearheaded California Electronic Communications Privacy Act requiring warrants for government access to electronic information
- Created model law for democratic oversight of surveillance systems, inspiring 25 laws protecting 17+ million people
- Co-authored Reader Privacy Act requiring 'super warrants' for government access to reading records
- Litigated civil liberties cases including NSA surveillance cases alongside EFF
- Led campaigns against social media surveillance and facial recognition by major tech companies
- Transition represents continuity in EFF's digital rights leadership during critical policy period
