# Russia Labels OVD-Info Rights Group 'Extremist' for Helping Protesters

**TL;DR:** Russia designated OVD-Info, a prominent rights group that provides legal aid to protesters and documents police abuses, as 'extremist' alongside 35 other organizations on June 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Russian authorities added OVD-Info and 35 other rights organizations to federal 'extremist' list on June 4, 2026
- OVD-Info has provided legal help to tens of thousands in freedom of assembly cases since 2011
- Group operates 24/7 hotline for abuse victims and has helped win 2,300+ European Court of Human Rights cases
- 'Extremist' designation carries up to 12 years prison for participation, 4 years for displaying symbols
- Authorities can freeze bank accounts of individuals suspected of involvement with listed groups
- Decision follows Supreme Court's classified April 9 ruling banning Memorial organization
- OVD-Info co-founder vows to continue operations despite the designation
