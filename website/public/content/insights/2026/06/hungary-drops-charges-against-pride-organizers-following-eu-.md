# Hungary Drops Charges Against Pride Organizers Following EU Court Ruling

**TL;DR:** Hungarian prosecutors dropped charges against Pride event organizers after European Court of Justice ruled Hungary's 2021 anti-LGBT law incompatible with EU law, signaling restoration of rule of law.

## Key Points

- Prosecutors dropped charges against Budapest mayor Gergely Karácsony for organizing 2025 Pride march
- Charges also dropped against Géza Buzás-Hábel, teacher/activist who organized rural Pride march in Pécs
- Both cases were prosecuted under Fidesz-era anti-LGBT legislation disguised as 'child protection'
- European Court of Justice ruled Hungary's 2021 anti-LGBT law incompatible with EU law
- Ruling came from European Commission challenge to Hungary's systematic rule of law attacks
- Decision signals Hungarian authorities' commitment to restoring rule of law
- Victory demonstrates successful resistance to state censorship of civil society organizing
- Hungary's parliament now required to bring domestic law into EU compliance
