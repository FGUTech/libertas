# Scientists Ejected from Diabetes Conference for Distributing Critical Editorial

**TL;DR:** Five prominent diabetes researchers were physically removed from the American Diabetes Association conference for distributing copies of a published editorial criticizing Trump administration attacks on scientific research.

## Key Points

- Five leading scientists ejected from American Diabetes Association annual meeting in New Orleans
- Scientists were distributing reprints of editorial published in Diabetes Care criticizing Trump administration's attacks on research
- Those removed included journal editor-in-chief Steven Kahn and former ADA president Desmond Schatz
- Incident occurred outside room where NIH director Jay Bhattacharya was scheduled to speak (he cancelled)
- Conference organizers physically removed scientists and revoked their credentials
- Scientists characterize incident as censorship of legitimate academic discourse
- Demonstrates concerning precedent for suppression of dissenting voices in scientific community
