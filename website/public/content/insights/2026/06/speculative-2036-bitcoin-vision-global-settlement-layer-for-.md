# Speculative 2036 Bitcoin Vision: Global Settlement Layer for Stablecoins and AI Commerce

**TL;DR:** Bitcoin Magazine piece envisions 2036 scenario where Bitcoin serves as invisible settlement infrastructure for stablecoins and AI agent transactions, with self-custody becoming default through better wallet design.

## Key Points

- Speculative article by David Marcus describes fictional 2036 where Bitcoin functions as 'TCP/IP of money'
- Envisions trillions moving daily on Bitcoin rails via stablecoins pegged to local currencies
- Describes 'Spark' wallet enabling single-address custody of dollars, bitcoin, and local currencies
- Claims self-custody becomes dominant when convenience matches traditional banking
- Predicts AI agents converge on Bitcoin for cross-jurisdictional settlements
- Suggests people save in Bitcoin by default, spend in stablecoins on same infrastructure
- Pure speculation presented as future fact with no verification or evidence
- Note: This is entirely fictional future scenario, not current development
