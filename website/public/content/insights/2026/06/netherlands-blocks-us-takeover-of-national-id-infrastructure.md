# Netherlands Blocks US Takeover of National ID Infrastructure on Digital Sovereignty Grounds

**TL;DR:** Dutch government blocked €100M acquisition of Solvinity (DigiD infrastructure provider) by US firm Kyndryl, citing CLOUD Act risks and marking first American takeover rejection on public interest grounds.

## Key Points

- Netherlands blocked Kyndryl's €100M takeover of Solvinity on May 25, 2026
- Solvinity manages cloud infrastructure for DigiD national ID system used for tax, medical, pension access
- Primary concern: US CLOUD Act would subject Dutch citizen data to American jurisdiction bypassing GDPR
- Bureau for Investment Screening ruled takeover posed 'risk to public interest' - first US rejection
- Decision follows months of opposition from citizens, journalists, privacy activists
- Part of broader EU digital sovereignty movement - Netherlands investing in STACKIT and KPN-Thales sovereign clouds
- Demonstrates concrete government action to protect critical digital infrastructure from foreign control
- Proton research shows 81% of Dutch public companies depend on American tech services
