# White House Frames Crypto Clarity Act as 'Pro-Enforcement' Bill

**TL;DR:** White House crypto adviser Patrick Witt defends the Clarity Act as a 'pro-enforcement bill' despite industry pushback, as lawmakers rush to pass the legislation.

## Key Points

- White House crypto adviser Patrick Witt publicly defended the Clarity Act
- Witt framed the bill as 'pro-enforcement' rather than pro-industry
- The characterization comes despite pushback from crypto advocates
- Lawmakers are reportedly racing to pass the bill
- White House positioning suggests focus on regulatory control over innovation
- Administration's framing indicates potential restrictions on crypto activities
- Timing suggests urgency around finalizing crypto regulatory framework
