# US Treasury Sanctions Iran's Largest Crypto Exchange Nobitex

**TL;DR:** US Treasury sanctioned Nobitex, which handled over half of Iran's crypto inflows in 2023, as part of broader crackdown on Iranian cryptocurrency infrastructure.

## Key Points

- US Treasury sanctioned Iranian crypto exchange Nobitex
- Nobitex processed more than 50% of Iran's crypto inflows in 2023
- Sanctions part of broader 'Economic Fury' campaign targeting Iranian financial infrastructure
- Demonstrates crypto's role as sanctions circumvention tool for sovereign nations
- Treasury action acknowledges crypto's effectiveness in bypassing traditional banking restrictions
- Sets precedent for targeting crypto infrastructure in sanctioned jurisdictions
- May drive further decentralization of Iranian crypto activity
