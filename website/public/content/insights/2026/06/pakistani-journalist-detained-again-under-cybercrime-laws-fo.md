# Pakistani Journalist Detained Again Under Cybercrime Laws for Kashmir Reporting

**TL;DR:** Pakistani authorities re-arrested journalist Sohrab Barkat for YouTube reports on Kashmir protests, seizing devices and charging him under cybercrime laws that criminalize 'false information.'

## Key Points

- Journalist Sohrab Barkat arrested June 5, 2026 at home in Islamabad for Kashmir reporting
- National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA) seized phone and filed charges under Pakistan's Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA)
- PECA criminalizes 'fake or false' information with up to 3 years prison
- Charges stem from YouTube report about protests by Jammu Kashmir Joint Awami Action Committee (JAAC)
- JAAC was banned under anti-terrorism laws same day as Barkat's arrest
- Government imposed internet disruptions during Kashmir protests that killed 15+ people
- Barkat previously detained 100 days (Nov 2025-March 2026) without charges
- Pattern shows systematic use of vague cybercrime laws to suppress independent journalism
