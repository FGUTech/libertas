# Russian Ruble Stablecoin Processes $110B Despite Western Sanctions

**TL;DR:** Russian ruble-backed stablecoin A7A5 has processed over $110 billion in transactions, demonstrating effective circumvention of Western financial sanctions through cryptocurrency infrastructure.

## Key Points

- A7A5 ruble-backed stablecoin processed over $110 billion in transactions according to CertiK analysis
- Transaction volume occurred despite comprehensive Western sanctions on Russian financial system
- Demonstrates real-world effectiveness of stablecoins for sanctions circumvention
- Shows cryptocurrency infrastructure maintaining financial sovereignty under pressure
- CertiK blockchain analytics firm provided transaction data verification
- Represents significant scale of alternative payment rails outside traditional banking
- Evidence of resilient financial infrastructure built on decentralized protocols

## Deep Dive

## Context

The $110 billion transaction volume for A7A5 represents a substantial real-world demonstration of cryptocurrency's ability to maintain financial operations under extreme regulatory pressure. Western sanctions have targeted Russia's access to SWIFT and traditional banking infrastructure, creating demand for alternative payment systems.

## Technical Implications

This volume suggests A7A5 has achieved meaningful adoption as financial infrastructure rather than speculative trading. Ruble-backed stablecoins provide price stability while maintaining independence from Western-controlled payment networks. The scale indicates sophisticated on/off-ramp infrastructure and merchant adoption.

## Censorship Resistance in Practice

The continued operation and growth of A7A5 demonstrates the practical limits of financial censorship when alternatives exist. Traditional sanctions rely on chokepoints in the legacy banking system that decentralized protocols can route around.

## What This Means for Builders

This case study validates the product-market fit for censorship-resistant payment infrastructure during geopolitical tensions. It shows that sovereignty-focused stablecoins can achieve scale when there's genuine need to circumvent traditional financial controls.
