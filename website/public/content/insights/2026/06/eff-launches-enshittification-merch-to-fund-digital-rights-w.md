# EFF Launches Enshittification Merch to Fund Digital Rights Work

**TL;DR:** EFF releases merchandise featuring Cory Doctorow's 'enshittification' emoji under Creative Commons license, with proceeds funding digital rights advocacy against surveillance capitalism and tech monopolies.

## Key Points

- EFF launches official enshittification emoji merchandise (stickers $5, pins $10)
- Design by Devin Washburn released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license
- Cory Doctorow released high-res files on Wikimedia Commons, Flickr, and Internet Archive
- All merchandise proceeds go directly to EFF's digital rights work
- Enshittification framework addresses surveillance business models and privacy erosion
- Visual serves as organizing tool and signal for understanding internet decay
- CC license allows anyone to create derivative works commercially with attribution
