# Pentagon Uses AI-Generated Content Mill to Spread Propaganda in Latin America

**TL;DR:** Investigation reveals U.S. military operates La Tilde, an AI-powered propaganda site targeting Latin American audiences with pro-Pentagon content disguised as independent journalism.

## Key Points

- Pentagon launched 'La Tilde,' an AI-generated news site targeting Latin America with pro-U.S. military content
- Site operated by U.S. Special Operations Command South (SOCSOUTH) with minimal disclosure of government funding
- Content includes fictional articles praising U.S. operations like 'capturing Nicolás Maduro' alongside personal finance tips
- AI detection tools confirmed multiple articles were machine-generated, representing new model for rapid propaganda deployment
- Planned country-specific versions for Ecuador, El Salvador, Guyana, Honduras, Jamaica, Panama, and Peru
- Content promotes U.S. military partnerships while downplaying sovereignty concerns and historical controversies
- Likely operated by defense contractor General Dynamics IT, following pattern of previous propaganda networks
- Represents evolution of Obama-era Trans-Regional Web Initiative using modern AI tools for influence operations
