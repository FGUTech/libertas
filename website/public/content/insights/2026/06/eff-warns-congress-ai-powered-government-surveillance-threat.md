# EFF Warns Congress: AI-Powered Government Surveillance Threatens Constitutional Rights

**TL;DR:** EFF testified to Congress that government adoption of AI for mass surveillance would supercharge unconstitutional civil liberties violations, calling for strong safeguards before deployment.

## Key Points

- EFF Senior Policy Analyst Dr. Matthew Guariglia testified to House Homeland Security Subcommittee on AI surveillance threats
- Warning focused on generative AI being used for mass government surveillance purposes
- Highlighted how government secrecy plus proprietary AI creates accountability gap when models make mistakes
- Cited examples of AI failures including DHS training error that sent recruits to field without proper preparation
- Emphasized unknown scope of AI mistakes due to classification preventing public oversight
- Guariglia stated the issue is 'how do we rein in the agencies that would unleash AI on the American public'
- Testimony called for strong Constitutional safeguards before government AI adoption
