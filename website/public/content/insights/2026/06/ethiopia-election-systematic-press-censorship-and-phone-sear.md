# Ethiopia Election: Systematic Press Censorship and Phone Searches Target Journalists

**TL;DR:** Ethiopian authorities are detaining journalists, searching their phones, and revoking media licenses ahead of June elections, creating what reporters call a climate of fear that has crippled independent election coverage.

## Key Points

- Journalist Bewket Abebe detained for 3 days after authorities searched his phone and found photos from contested territory
- Security personnel searched journalist's device without warrant in northern border town of Humera
- Addis Standard managing editor Million Beyene forcibly disappeared for nearly two weeks by unidentified men
- Multiple media outlets had licenses revoked, including Addis Standard and threats to The Reporter Magazine
- Journalists report phone surveillance, informal warnings, and pressure from regional officials
- CPJ interviewed 11 journalists documenting systematic intimidation ahead of June 1 election
- Press freedom restrictions concentrated in conflict zones of Tigray, Amhara, and Oromia regions
- Many journalists now working from home or have fled the profession entirely
