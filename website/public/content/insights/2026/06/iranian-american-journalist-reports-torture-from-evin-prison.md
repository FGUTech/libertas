# Iranian-American Journalist Reports Torture from Evin Prison in Smuggled Recording

**TL;DR:** Jailed Iranian-American journalist Reza Valizadeh smuggled out voice memo from Evin Prison detailing physical torture, psychological abuse, and medical neglect, highlighting risks faced by press freedom advocates.

## Key Points

- Reza Valizadeh, former Radio Farda journalist, recorded voice memo from Tehran's Evin Prison describing torture and medical neglect
- Recording obtained and aired by CBS News, providing rare firsthand account of prison conditions
- Valizadeh sentenced to 10 years in prison on charges linked to his journalistic work since September 2024
- Reports 'physical pressure and mental torture' and denial of medical services for chronic asthma
- Prison conditions worsened after Israeli military strikes on Evin facility in June 2025
- Committee to Protect Journalists calls for urgent US diplomatic intervention
- Case demonstrates successful information smuggling from high-security detention facility
