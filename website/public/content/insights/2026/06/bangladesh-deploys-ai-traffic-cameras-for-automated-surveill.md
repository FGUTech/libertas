# Bangladesh Deploys AI Traffic Cameras for Automated Surveillance and Case Filing in Dhaka

**TL;DR:** Bangladesh launched AI-powered traffic cameras in Dhaka that automatically scan license plates, file cases, and send prosecution notices without human involvement—marking the country's first fully automated digital enforcement system.

## Key Points

- Dhaka Metropolitan Police deployed AI cameras at major intersections starting May 7, 2026
- System automatically scans plates, cross-references BRTA database, and files cases for violations
- Over 1,000 automated cases generated in first 10 days of operation
- Plans to expand to 500 camera locations across Dhaka within six months
- Cases filed against vehicle owners regardless of who was driving
- System cannot detect unregistered vehicles like battery-powered rickshaws with no plates
- Raises concerns about comprehensive vehicle tracking and removal of human discretion in enforcement
