# US Congress Moves to Permanently Integrate Israeli AI Surveillance Tech Into Military

**TL;DR:** House defense bill includes Section 224 creating permanent framework to integrate Israeli AI, autonomous systems, and surveillance tech into US military operations, raising concerns about domestic surveillance capabilities.

## Key Points

- National Defense Authorization Act Section 224 would permanently integrate US-Israeli defense technology cooperation
- Framework covers AI, autonomous systems, cyberwarfare, biotechnology, and surveillance technologies
- Military experts warn Israeli AI surveillance systems used against Palestinians could target US citizens
- Unlike traditional aid, creates integrated research, procurement, and manufacturing processes
- Bipartisan opposition from Reps. Thomas Massie (R-KY) and Ro Khanna (D-CA) failed in committee
- Section mirrors failed FUTURES Act backed by AIPAC and pro-Israel lobby groups
- Pentagon refuses to comment amid reports of Israeli espionage concerns
- Designed to replace $3.8 billion annual military aid with technology partnership model
