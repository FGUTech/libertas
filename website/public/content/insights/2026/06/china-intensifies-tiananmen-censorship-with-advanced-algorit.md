# China Intensifies Tiananmen Censorship with Advanced Algorithmic Filtering

**TL;DR:** Chinese authorities are deploying sophisticated censorship algorithms that can flag indirect references like 'one banana and four apples' as Tiananmen symbols, while expanding surveillance to license plates and social gatherings.

## Key Points

- China's censorship algorithms now detect abstract references to Tiananmen, including fruit arrangements mimicking 'tank man' imagery
- Hong Kong car owner fled country after receiving threatening letters about license plate 'US 8964' (June 4, 1989 date)
- Authorities blocked Tiananmen Mothers' New Year gathering for first time since 2009, escalating surveillance of victim families
- Hong Kong national security trial targets commemoration organizers with 10-year prison sentences under subversion charges
- Leaked military trial footage from 1990 shows general who refused orders, demonstrating ongoing information control failures
- Police detained 12 Hong Kong residents in 2025 for 'suspicious behavior' during anniversary period
- Diaspora groups organizing commemorations across 30+ cities in 7 countries as mainland/Hong Kong spaces eliminated
- Victoria Park now hosts 'patriotic food carnival' during massacre anniversary dates as counter-narrative operation
