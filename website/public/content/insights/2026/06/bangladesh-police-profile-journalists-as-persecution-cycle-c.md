# Bangladesh: Police Profile Journalists as Persecution Cycle Continues Under New Government

**TL;DR:** CPJ reports Bangladesh's new PM Tarique Rahman continues cycle of journalist persecution after 100 days in office, with police conducting background checks and profiling journalists nationwide.

## Key Points

- Police conducting background checks and profiling journalists across Bangladesh [CPJ report]
- Three governments in under two years have each persecuted journalists aligned with previous administrations
- At least 25 journalists under investigation by International Crimes Tribunal for editorial work
- 282 journalists facing politically motivated cases as of May 2026 [Editors' Council]
- Cyber Protection Act 2026 retains vague definitions enabling press repression
- Multiple journalists detained since August 2024, including Farzana Rupa and Shakil Ahmed from Ekattor TV
- Major newsrooms attacked during December 2025 unrest, including Prothom Alo and The Daily Star offices burned
- Anti-Terrorism Act 2009 being used to prosecute journalists for editorial work
- Pattern shows systematic surveillance and legal weaponization against press regardless of ruling party
