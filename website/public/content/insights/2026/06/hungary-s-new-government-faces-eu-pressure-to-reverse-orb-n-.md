# Hungary's New Government Faces EU Pressure to Reverse Orbán's Media Capture and Spyware Use

**TL;DR:** After defeating Orbán, Hungary's new PM Péter Magyar must dismantle 16 years of media capture and surveillance. CPJ urges EU to push for press freedom reforms and end spyware targeting of journalists.

## Key Points

- Péter Magyar's Tisza party defeated Viktor Orbán's Fidesz in April 2026 elections after 16 years in power
- Committee to Protect Journalists (CPJ) calls on EU to pressure new government for media freedom reforms
- Orbán administration turned Hungarian media into 'de facto propaganda mouthpieces' through systematic capture
- Previous government deployed spyware against investigative reporters, undermining source confidentiality
- EU's European Media Freedom Act and anti-SLAPP Directive provide framework for media reform
- New government committed to rule-of-law reforms required to unblock frozen EU funds
- Independent journalists endured 'massive psychological and financial pressure' under Orbán regime
- European Parliament's PEGA Committee previously documented spyware abuses for 'national security' claims
