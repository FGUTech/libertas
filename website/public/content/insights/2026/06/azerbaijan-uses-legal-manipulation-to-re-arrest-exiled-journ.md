# Azerbaijan Uses Legal Manipulation to Re-Arrest Exiled Journalist After Forced Return

**TL;DR:** Azerbaijani journalist Afgan Sadigov was re-arrested after authorities terminated then revived criminal charges to facilitate his forced deportation from Georgia, illustrating sophisticated transnational repression tactics.

## Key Points

- Afgan Sadigov, editor-in-chief of Azel.TV, detained by masked men in Baku on June 8, 2026
- Azerbaijan initially terminated criminal case in April 2026 to bypass European Court of Human Rights protection order
- Georgia deported Sadigov despite binding European Court interim measure protecting him from transfer
- Azerbaijani authorities then revived the same criminal case to justify re-arrest after his return
- Pattern demonstrates coordinated legal manipulation between Azerbaijan and Georgia to silence exile critic
- European Court's interim measures proved ineffective against administrative deportation workaround
- Case illustrates how authoritarian states coordinate across borders to target independent media
