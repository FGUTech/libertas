# Russian Journalist Detained for Telegram Comments, Faces 7 Years in Prison

**TL;DR:** Russian authorities detained journalist Aleksey Seregin for 'justifying terrorism' over a 2024 Telegram comment about Chechen independence, highlighting risks of using mainstream platforms under authoritarian regimes.

## Key Points

- Aleksey Seregin detained in Mari El Republic after beating, phone confiscation, and home search
- Charged with 'justifying terrorism' for 2024 Telegram chat comment about Chechen independence leader
- Faces up to 7 years under Article 205.2 of Russia's Criminal Code
- Runs Telegram channel 'Nothing Will Happen in Mari El' covering local corruption and human rights
- Previously designated 'foreign agent' by Russian Ministry of Justice in November 2024
- Only active independent journalist in Mari El Republic covering sensitive local issues
- Russia currently holds at least 29 journalists in connection with their work
- Case demonstrates risks of using non-encrypted platforms for sensitive communications
