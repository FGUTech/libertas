# Researchers Document 'Chilling Effect' of Government Surveillance on Free Speech

**TL;DR:** Citizen Lab researchers argue that surveillance and government threats are causing widespread self-censorship among Americans, creating a chilling effect that extends far beyond campus protests.

## Key Points

- Citizen Lab's Jon Penney and Bruce Schneier published op-ed on surveillance-driven self-censorship
- Researchers observe fewer protests despite strong opposition to current administration
- Define 'chilling effect' as behavioral tendency to self-censor due to perceived threats
- U.S. government systematically employs surveillance, personal threats, and power abuse
- Self-censorship pattern extends beyond campuses to broader population
- Authors argue resisting self-censorship must become the norm, not exception
- Analysis published in The Conversation, republished by Salon and McClatchy Media Group
