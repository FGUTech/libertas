# House Rushes Through Copyright Office Overhaul That Threatens Digital Rights

**TL;DR:** House passed H.R. 6028 restructuring the Copyright Office, making it more political and concentrating power in a presidential appointee, threatening fair use and circumvention rights.

## Key Points

- House passed H.R. 6028 via voice vote, removing Library of Congress oversight over Copyright Office
- Bill makes Register of Copyrights a presidential appointee confirmed by Senate
- Transfers DMCA Section 1201 rulemaking authority from Librarian to Register of Copyrights
- Copyright Office historically supported SOPA and consistently favors industry over public interest
- Section 1201 rulemaking determines when public can bypass digital locks for security research, repair, accessibility
- EFF and coalition urged against fast-tracking but Congress moved forward without hearings
- Changes politicize office that already wields significant influence over tech policy and digital rights
- Bill now goes to Senate where EFF urges rejection
