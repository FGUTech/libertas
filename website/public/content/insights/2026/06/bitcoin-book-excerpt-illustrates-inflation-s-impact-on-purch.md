# Bitcoin Book Excerpt Illustrates Inflation's Impact on Purchasing Power Over 40 Years

**TL;DR:** New book excerpt uses German ice cream prices to demonstrate how inflation destroyed 80% of purchasing power over 40 years, positioning Bitcoin's fixed supply as alternative.

## Key Points

- Alex von Frankenberg's book excerpt compares 1980s German wages to today's purchasing power
- Newspaper delivery work bought 33 ice cream scoops per hour in 1980s vs 6-8 scoops today
- Author calculates 80% loss in real purchasing power for workers over 40 years
- Global M2 money supply at $120 trillion creates $4.8 trillion annual purchasing power destruction at 4% inflation
- Book argues Bitcoin's 21 million coin cap prevents monetary debasement
- Currently 19.9 million Bitcoin exist with only 5% remaining to be mined over next 115 years
- Positions Bitcoin as hedge against currency devaluation affecting 90% of citizens
