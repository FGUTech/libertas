# Meta Used CIA/FBI-Linked Contractor for Smart Glasses Face Recognition Development

**TL;DR:** Meta partnered with Rank One Computing, a facial recognition firm with former CIA and FBI leadership, for internal development of smart glasses capabilities.

## Key Points

- Meta contracted Rank One Computing for face recognition technology development
- Rank One's board includes former CIA deputy director and former FBI science chief
- Technology being developed for Meta's smart glasses application
- Partnership represents convergence of consumer tech and intelligence community capabilities
- Raises concerns about normalization of surveillance in everyday devices
- Meta's smart glasses could enable widespread facial recognition deployment
- Intelligence community connections suggest potential dual-use applications
