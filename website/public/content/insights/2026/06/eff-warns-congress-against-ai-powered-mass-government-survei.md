# EFF Warns Congress Against AI-Powered Mass Government Surveillance

**TL;DR:** EFF testified to Congress that government adoption of AI without strong safeguards would supercharge unconstitutional mass surveillance and civil liberties violations.

## Key Points

- EFF Senior Policy Analyst Dr. Matthew Guariglia testified to House Homeland Security Subcommittee
- Warned that generative AI for mass government surveillance would supercharge civil liberties violations
- Highlighted government secrecy combined with proprietary AI prevents oversight of AI mistakes
- Cited AI errors including DHS training failures and false legal citations as examples
- Emphasized need for strong Constitutional safeguards before government AI adoption
- Guariglia stated the real issue is 'reining in agencies that would unleash AI on the American public'
- Testimony addresses cybersecurity and critical infrastructure protection contexts
