# US Govt Forces Anthropic to Shut Down AI Models Over Jailbreak Concerns

**TL;DR:** Anthropic immediately shut down its newly launched Mythos 5 and Fable 5 AI models after receiving a Commerce Department directive citing export control concerns and reported jailbreaks bypassing safety measures.

## Key Points

- Anthropic shut off Mythos 5 and Fable 5 models Friday night after Commerce Department directive
- Models were subject to new export controls restricting use outside the United States
- Administration cited concerns over jailbreaks bypassing safeguards for cybersecurity, chemistry, and biology prompts
- Government requested pause to allow 'national security apparatus' to be 'hardened' against threats
- Other Anthropic models remain accessible and unaffected by the shutdown
- Timeline for resolution suggested as 'next few weeks' according to administration sources
- Demonstrates government's ability to rapidly restrict AI tools deemed security risks
