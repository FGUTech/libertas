# Rwanda Arrests YouTube Journalists for Exposing Secret Detention Facility

**TL;DR:** Two journalists from YouTube channel Imbarutso ya Demokarasi were arrested and charged for reporting on Kwa Kabuga, an unofficial detention center in Kigali where authorities hold 'undesirable' people.

## Key Points

- Augustin Nsanzimana and Emmanuel Niyonshuti arrested for YouTube reporting on Kwa Kabuga detention facility
- Charged with 'publishing rumors that may incite fear' - a vague offense used to suppress criticism
- Denied legal counsel for first six days of detention, confirmed in pre-trial detention June 2
- Rwanda Investigation Bureau agents reportedly entered Nsanzimana's home May 1 before arrests
- Their YouTube video alleged detainee deaths inside the unofficial detention center
- Human Rights Watch has documented arbitrary detention and ill treatment at Kwa Kabuga facility
- Part of broader crackdown targeting YouTube journalists and opposition voices in Rwanda
- Academic YouTuber Aimable Karasira died in prison under questionable circumstances in May
