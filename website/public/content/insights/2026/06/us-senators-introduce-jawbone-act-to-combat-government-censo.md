# US Senators Introduce JAWBONE Act to Combat Government Censorship Pressure

**TL;DR:** Bipartisan JAWBONE Act would prohibit federal agencies from coercing tech platforms and broadcasters into restricting speech, creating legal remedies for victims of government 'jawboning' tactics.

## Key Points

- Senators Ted Cruz (R-TX) and Ron Wyden (D-OR) introduced the Justice Against Weaponized Bureaucratic Overreach to Networked Expression Act
- Bill prohibits federal agencies from coercing broadcasters, online services, or AI services to change content
- Creates private right of action allowing victims to recover compensatory damages in court
- Targets government pressure on social media firms, AI chatbot makers, and broadcasters
- Could apply to FCC Chairman Brendan Carr's pressure campaigns against TV networks
- Enables enforcement by both individuals and state attorneys general through civil actions
- Addresses 'jawboning' - indirect government censorship through pressure rather than direct regulation
