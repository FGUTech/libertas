# BLM Activist Vanishes in Prison System After Cross-Country Transfer

**TL;DR:** Incarcerated activist Malik Muhammad disappeared from tracking systems for weeks before surfacing 3,000 miles away in South Carolina, allegedly as retaliation for prison organizing efforts.

## Key Points

- Malik Muhammad, serving longest federal sentence of any 2020 BLM protester, vanished from Oregon prison tracking systems in March
- Muhammad reappeared weeks later at Kirkland Correctional Institute in South Carolina, 3,000 miles from Oregon
- Transfer allegedly violates plea agreement specifying service in Oregon state prison near supporters
- Attorney Lauren Regan cannot provide privileged legal counsel due to out-of-state licensing restrictions
- South Carolina DOC initially claimed no record of Muhammad in their custody system
- Prison Policy Initiative confirms people getting 'lost' in prison transfers is 'pretty common'
- Muhammad had been organizing potential class-action lawsuit against Oregon's solitary confinement practices
- Oregon DOC denies retaliation, cites 'Interstate Compact' review process for transfer
