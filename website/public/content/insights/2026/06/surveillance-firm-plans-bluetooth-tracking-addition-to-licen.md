# Surveillance Firm Plans Bluetooth Tracking Addition to License Plate Readers

**TL;DR:** Leonardo's SignalTrace technology would add Bluetooth device detection to ALPR cameras, enabling tracking of specific individuals via their phones and wearables, not just vehicles.

## Key Points

- Leonardo developing SignalTrace to add Bluetooth sensors to automatic license plate readers (ALPRs)
- Technology would capture unique identifiers from mobile phones, wearables, and other Bluetooth devices in vehicles
- Transforms ALPR systems from vehicle tracking to individual person tracking capabilities
- Would enable law enforcement to identify specific drivers and passengers through their devices
- ALPR cameras already widely deployed across the United States infrastructure
- Represents significant expansion of mass surveillance capabilities using existing camera networks
- Schneier notes smartphone data collection already exceeds this level of surveillance [Source]
