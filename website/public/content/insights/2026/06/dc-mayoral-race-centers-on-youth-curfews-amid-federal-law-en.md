# DC Mayoral Race Centers on Youth Curfews Amid Federal Law Enforcement Surge

**TL;DR:** DC mayoral candidates split on police-enforced teen curfews as Trump admin deploys federal agents citywide, with debate over whether local crackdowns prevent or enable federal overreach.

## Key Points

- DC mayoral candidates McDuffie and Lewis George disagree on youth curfew policies
- McDuffie argues curfews will prevent federal intervention; Lewis George opposes due to federal agent presence
- Trump's US Attorney Jeanine Pirro threatens 'summer surge' targeting teens and prosecuting parents
- Research shows teen curfews don't reduce crime but increase arrests, especially of Black youth
- 2015 DC study found juvenile curfews actually increased gun violence rates
- Nearly 20 federal agencies now deployed across DC, increasing arrest likelihood
- 71% of DC voters support curfews despite Lewis George leading polls by 11 points
- Debate reflects broader tension between local sovereignty and federal control
