# Anthropic Takes Funding from UAE Surveillance State While Condemning AI Authoritarianism

**TL;DR:** Anthropic raised $95B from UAE's MGX fund while publishing anti-authoritarian AI policy, exposing double standards as UAE uses AI for surveillance and repression.

## Key Points

- Anthropic raised $30B in February and $65B in May from MGX, UAE's sovereign wealth fund
- Company published policy paper claiming 'democracies must lead AI development' to prevent authoritarianism
- UAE receives 18/100 freedom score, outlaws political parties, free press, and assembly
- MGX chair Sheikh Tahnoun bin Zayed Al Nahyan linked to surveillance campaigns against dissidents
- Tahnoun connected to ToTok messaging app used for covert government surveillance [Source]
- G42 (Tahnoun's AI firm) allegedly analyzed all ToTok voice, video, text chats with AI for 'suspicious activity'
- Anthropic's valuation now at $965B with authoritarian funding while condemning Chinese AI surveillance
- Exposes contradiction between stated democratic values and willingness to accept authoritarian capital
