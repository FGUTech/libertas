# Mali Arrests Journalists Under Cybercrime Law for Factual Reporting

**TL;DR:** Mali's military junta arrested two journalists under cybercrime laws for stating basic facts - one for criticizing misuse of cyber legislation, another for reporting territorial control by armed groups.

## Key Points

- Journalists Chahana Takiou and Abdramane Keïta arrested in Mali on June 8-9, 2026
- Takiou charged with 'undermining state credibility' for criticizing cybercrime law misuse at media forum
- Keïta charged with 'regionalist offense' for stating JNIM controls northern city of Kidal
- Both prosecuted under cybercrime unit rather than press laws, bypassing journalist protections
- Mali's 2019 cybercrime law criminalizes vague online 'threats' and 'insults' with up to 10-year penalties
- Pattern follows broader crackdown since 2020 military coup including media bans and civil society dissolution
- Demonstrates how authoritarian regimes weaponize digital legislation against factual reporting
