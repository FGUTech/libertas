# 200+ Crypto Companies Push Senate to Vote on Digital Asset Clarity Act

**TL;DR:** Coalition of 200+ companies including Coinbase, Circle, and a16z urge Senate leaders to bring the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act to floor vote, seeking regulatory framework to define SEC vs CFTC jurisdiction over digital assets.

## Key Points

- Stand With Crypto and 200+ companies sent letter to Senate leaders Thune and Schumer on June 7
- Coalition includes Coinbase, Circle, Ripple, Kraken, Andreessen Horowitz, Binance.US, and Uniswap Labs
- Digital Asset Market Clarity Act passed House 294-134, cleared Senate Banking Committee 15-9
- Bill aims to resolve SEC/CFTC regulatory jurisdiction dispute with clear token definitions
- Remaining obstacles include DeFi provisions, ethics language, and anti-money laundering concerns
- Galaxy Digital estimates 60% chance of becoming law but notes tight timeline before August recess
- Coalition frames as test of American leadership vs offshore jurisdictions with weaker protections
