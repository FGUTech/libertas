# NY Court Case Challenges State Claims to 'Lost' Bitcoin Wallets

**TL;DR:** New York lawsuit seeks ownership of nearly 40,000 Bitcoin wallets; amicus brief argues state lost-and-found laws can't claim assets controlled by private keys.

## Key Points

- NY court case involves attempted state seizure of nearly 40,000 Bitcoin wallets
- Amicus brief filed by attorney Ian R. Cohen challenges the legal basis
- Brief argues NY lost-and-found statute cannot apply to Bitcoin controlled by private keys
- Core principle at stake: private key control equals ownership
- July hearing scheduled to consider the amicus brief
- Case could set important precedent for Bitcoin self-custody rights
- Challenges government attempts to claim 'abandoned' cryptocurrency
