# Azerbaijan Seeks 16-Year Prison Terms for Independent Media Workers Over Foreign Funding

**TL;DR:** Azerbaijani prosecutors seek up to 16 years in prison for 9 journalists and media workers from Toplum TV and affiliated organizations, charged with using Western donor funds in violation of local laws.

## Key Points

- Nine defendants linked to Toplum TV face 13-16 year prison sentences on financial crime charges
- Accused of forming 'organized criminal group' to obtain Western donor funds for four organizations including Toplum TV and fact-checking platform Fakt Yoxla
- Part of broader crackdown following Azerbaijan's 2023 Nagorno-Karabakh military campaign, targeting dozens of journalists and civil society groups
- Azerbaijani law requires state approval for foreign grants, but independent experts say authorities refuse to register independent organizations
- European Court of Human Rights ruled similar violations should carry fines, not criminal sanctions
- Azerbaijan ranks as world's 6th-worst jailer of journalists with at least 24 currently detained
- Previous cases saw 6 Abzas Media journalists sentenced to 7.5-9 years on similar charges
- Demonstrates weaponization of financial regulations to suppress independent media and civil society
