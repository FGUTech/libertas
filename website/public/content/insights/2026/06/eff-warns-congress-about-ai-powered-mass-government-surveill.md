# EFF Warns Congress About AI-Powered Mass Government Surveillance Risks

**TL;DR:** EFF testified to Congress that government adoption of AI for mass surveillance would supercharge unconstitutional civil liberties violations, citing AI errors and lack of transparency.

## Key Points

- EFF's Dr. Matthew Guariglia testified to House Homeland Security Subcommittee on AI surveillance risks
- Warned that generative AI for mass government surveillance would supercharge constitutional violations
- Highlighted AI error track record, including DHS training mistake that sent recruits to field improperly
- Government secrecy plus proprietary AI black boxes prevent accountability for mistakes
- AI errors could seriously impact critical infrastructure cybersecurity and individual lives
- Emphasized need for strong safeguards before government AI adoption
- Framed issue as reining in agencies that would 'unleash AI on the American public'
