# California's A.B. 412 Would Create Impossible AI Compliance Burden, Crush Open Source

**TL;DR:** California's A.B. 412 demands AI developers identify all copyrighted training data, but EFF warns this is technically impossible and would lock in Big Tech monopolies while crushing small developers and open-source projects.

## Key Points

- A.B. 412 requires AI developers to identify and disclose all copyrighted works used in training
- EFF filed opposition letter calling the requirements practically impossible to implement
- No machine-readable list of copyrighted works exists at U.S. Copyright Office
- Bill covers any developer making AI models available to Californians, not just Big Tech
- Large companies can hire compliance teams; small developers and open-source projects cannot
- Federal courts are already adjudicating AI fair use questions with some ruling in favor of training
- Bill would discourage innovation while cementing dominance of largest AI companies
- Rights holders already have powerful federal copyright protections available
