# Bangladesh's Cycle of Press Repression Continues Under New Government

**TL;DR:** After 100 days in office, PM Tarique Rahman's government continues systematic persecution of journalists through detention, surveillance, and repressive cyber laws despite election pledges to protect press freedom.

## Key Points

- Committee to Protect Journalists criticizes PM Tarique Rahman's government after 100 days for failing to break cycle of press persecution
- 282 journalists currently face politically motivated cases, with dozens detained since August 2024 [Source: CPJ]
- Police conducting background checks and profiling journalists across Bangladesh [Source: The Daily Star]
- International Crimes Tribunal being weaponized against 25+ journalists for editorial work, charging them with genocide/crimes against humanity
- Cyber Protection Act 2026 retains vague definitions enabling press repression from previous cyber laws
- Major newsrooms Prothom Alo and The Daily Star attacked and set on fire during December 2025 unrest
- Pattern of criminalizing journalists persists across three government transitions since August 2024
