# Proton Releases Journalist Security Guide for 2026 FIFA World Cup Coverage

**TL;DR:** Proton published comprehensive security guidance for 50,000 journalists covering the 2026 World Cup, addressing US travel bans, warrantless device searches, and digital protection strategies.

## Key Points

- Guide targets estimated 50,000 journalists covering 2026 FIFA World Cup across US, Mexico, and Canada
- US travel bans affect citizens from 12+ countries including Iran, Libya, Somalia, Sudan, and Yemen
- CBP can search devices without warrants at borders, creating surveillance risks for journalists
- Committee to Protect Journalists warns of 'unpredictable environment' with inconsistent enforcement
- Recommended protections include end-to-end encryption, password managers, 2FA, and cloud backups
- Legal resources provided: CPJ emergency hotline (1-206-590-6191) and Press Freedom Tracker
- $625 million in federal security funding allocated to 11 US host cities
- Proton offers business discounts to news organizations for enhanced protection
