# Citizen Lab Director Details Greek Predator Spyware Scandal Investigation

**TL;DR:** Citizen Lab's Ron Deibert spoke about exposing Intellexa's Predator spyware use against Greek journalists and politicians, triggering national and EU investigations.

## Key Points

- Ron Deibert gave keynote at Athens event about Greek spyware scandal investigation
- Citizen Lab exposed use of Intellexa's Predator spyware against Greek journalists and political figures
- Investigation triggered both national and European Union investigations
- Deibert credited investigative journalists as 'real heroes' for exposing closed-door surveillance
- Case demonstrates commercial spyware threat to press freedom and democratic institutions
- Event hosted by Eteron think tank highlights ongoing European concern over surveillance overreach
- Represents successful model for technical research organizations supporting investigative journalism

## Deep Dive

## Context and Significance

The Greek spyware scandal represents one of Europe's most significant surveillance controversies, with Citizen Lab's technical analysis providing crucial evidence of Predator spyware deployment against media and political targets. The case has drawn comparisons to Watergate due to its scope and political implications, with investigations reaching the highest levels of Greek government.

## Technical and Investigative Model

Citizen Lab's approach in this case demonstrates the critical role technical research organizations play in supporting investigative journalism. Their forensic analysis of mobile devices and network infrastructure provided the technical evidence necessary to substantiate claims of surveillance, creating a reproducible methodology for exposing commercial spyware operations.

## Broader Implications for Freedom Tech

The Greek case highlights both the threat commercial spyware poses to democratic institutions and the effectiveness of combining technical research with investigative journalism. Deibert's emphasis on journalists as 'heroes' underscores the symbiotic relationship between technical capability and journalistic investigation in exposing state surveillance overreach.

## What This Means for the Ecosystem

The success in Greece provides a template for other regions facing similar surveillance threats, while also demonstrating how technical organizations can effectively support press freedom through rigorous forensic analysis and public disclosure of findings.
