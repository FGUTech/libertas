# DRC: Secret Cyber Defense Unit Disappears Dissidents, HRW Documents Network of Detention Sites

**TL;DR:** Human Rights Watch documents systematic repression in DRC where National Cyber Defense Council arbitrarily arrests activists, holding them in secret locations without charges or legal access.

## Key Points

- National Cyber Defense Council (CNC) operates secret detention network in Kinshasa targeting dissidents
- Activists and political opponents forcibly disappeared, held without formal charges or lawyer access
- Families blindfolded when visiting detainees, locations kept secret from relatives
- HRW Executive Director Philippe Bolopion met with PM Judith Suminwa and other officials May 17-21, 2026
- Government claims detainees held in villas while investigations finalized before judicial transfer
- Officials promise CNC reforms requiring judicial police presence for future arrests
- Crackdown parallels Joseph Kabila era repression, violates Tshisekedi's pledges to end such practices
- Broader context includes M23 occupation in eastern Congo and media/opposition crackdowns
