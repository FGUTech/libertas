# OONI Joins Quadratic Funding Campaign for Internet Freedom Projects

**TL;DR:** OONI and 9 other internet freedom projects launched a quadratic funding campaign to secure decentralized funding, citing sustainability risks.

## Key Points

- OONI joined 10 like-minded projects in new quadratic funding campaign for internet freedom
- Campaign hosted by Tor Project at internetfreedom.torproject.org
- OONI states their ability to continue work is "at risk" without funding
- Quadratic funding allows small donations to have amplified impact through matching
- Represents shift toward decentralized funding models for freedom tech projects
- Campaign targets crypto-native funding community for internet freedom tools
- Highlights ongoing sustainability challenges facing critical infrastructure projects
