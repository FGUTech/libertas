# Kazakhstan Forces Anti-War Orthodox Priest Into Psychiatric Detention

**TL;DR:** Kazakhstan authorities transferred activist priest Yakov Vorontsov to psychiatric facility on dubious drug charges, following years of pressure over his anti-war stance and efforts to establish independent Orthodox community.

## Key Points

- Yakov Vorontsov, defrocked Orthodox priest, forcibly transferred to psychiatric facility on May 25, 2026
- Originally arrested February 13 on drug charges that he claims were fabricated with planted evidence
- Targeted for opposing Russia's Ukraine war and attempting to register independent Orthodox community
- Court ordered psychiatric detention as 'security measure' without defendant or lawyer present
- Authorities denied family visits, confiscated religious materials, forcibly shaved head/beard
- Pattern follows previous criminal investigation for 'inciting religious discord' closed for lack of evidence
- UN experts raised concerns about the case in March 31 communication to Kazakhstan government
- HRW documents this as part of Kazakhstan's growing use of psychiatric detention for political repression
