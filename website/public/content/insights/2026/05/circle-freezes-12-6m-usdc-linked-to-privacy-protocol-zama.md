# Circle Freezes $12.6M USDC Linked to Privacy Protocol Zama

**TL;DR:** Circle froze $12.6M USDC connected to privacy protocol Zama, reportedly due to unrelated civil court case, highlighting centralized stablecoin control risks.

## Key Points

- Circle froze $12.6 million USDC associated with privacy protocol Zama
- Freeze reportedly connected to ongoing civil court case unrelated to Zama itself
- Information comes from blockchain analyst ZachXBT's investigation
- Demonstrates centralized control mechanisms in major stablecoin infrastructure
- Highlights surveillance risks for privacy-focused protocols using traditional crypto rails
- Zama is known for fully homomorphic encryption (FHE) blockchain solutions
- Incident underscores importance of censorship-resistant payment alternatives
