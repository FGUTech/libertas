# Tunisia Escalates Media Crackdown with New Journalist Convictions

**TL;DR:** Tunisia convicted lawyer-commentator Sonia Dahmani under cybercrime law for criticizing prison conditions, part of systematic repression targeting journalists and blocking protest coverage.

## Key Points

- Sonia Dahmani sentenced to 2 years for 'spreading false news' under Decree Law 54 over 2023 prison criticism
- Dahmani previously jailed 18 months, faces five separate cases for public commentary
- Pattern of escalating repression under President Kais Saied includes multiple journalist prosecutions
- Authorities recently sentenced journalists Mourad Zghidi and Borhen Bsaies, continuing harassment of others
- Police prevented journalists from covering Tunisian Bar Association protest on May 19
- Government using cybercrime law to systematically prosecute critical voices
- Demonstrates urgent need for secure communications and censorship-resistant reporting tools in Tunisia
