# UK Sanctions HTX Exchange Over Alleged Russia Sanction Circumvention

**TL;DR:** The UK sanctioned HTX (formerly Huobi Global) crypto exchange, claiming it's being exploited by Russia to bypass existing financial sanctions.

## Key Points

- UK government imposed sanctions on HTX cryptocurrency exchange (formerly Huobi Global)
- Authorities claim the exchange is being exploited by Russia to circumvent UK sanctions
- Part of broader UK crackdown on companies allegedly facilitating sanctions evasion
- Demonstrates real-world use of crypto infrastructure for financial sovereignty
- Government action confirms effectiveness of crypto for bypassing traditional financial controls
- Sets precedent for sanctioning crypto exchanges based on usage patterns rather than direct violations
- Highlights ongoing tension between permissionless finance and state financial controls
