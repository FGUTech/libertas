# California Expands Age-Gating to Browsers/Websites, Exempts Open Source

**TL;DR:** CA's AB 1856 exempts open-source OS from age verification while expanding mandatory age-bracketing from OS/app stores to all browsers and websites.

## Key Points

- AB 1856 amends last year's AB 1043 Digital Age Assurance Act with mixed results
- New exemption excludes open-source operating systems from age-bracketing requirements
- Bill expands age verification mandates to cover all web browsers and websites
- Open-source exemption applies to software with copy/redistribute/modify rights
- EFF warns expansion creates 'nearly impossible' barrier to avoiding age gates
- Age-bracketing pressures companies toward ID checks and biometric scanning
- Bill passed Assembly 68-1, now moves to California Senate for consideration
- Ambiguity remains on commercial products incorporating open-source components
