# Court Order Freezes $12.6M in Zama's Confidential USDC Contract

**TL;DR:** Circle froze $12.6M in Zama's confidential USDC contract following a court order related to Overnight Finance lawsuit, highlighting vulnerability of privacy tech to state intervention.

## Key Points

- Court-ordered freeze trapped $12.6 million in Zama's confidential USDC smart contract
- Circle executed the freeze as part of lawsuit involving Overnight Finance
- Zama CEO Rand Hindi says protocol was "caught in crossfire" of legal dispute
- Demonstrates centralized stablecoin vulnerability even within privacy-preserving protocols
- Zama focuses on confidential computing and encrypted blockchain transactions
- Incident exposes tension between financial privacy tech and traditional legal systems
- Team investigating the freeze but limited recourse due to Circle's centralized control
