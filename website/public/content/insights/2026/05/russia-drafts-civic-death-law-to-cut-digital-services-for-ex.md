# Russia Drafts 'Civic Death' Law to Cut Digital Services for Exiled Critics

**TL;DR:** Russia's parliament reviews draft law denying exiled critics access to digital government services, banking, and consular support - creating urgent need for sovereign identity solutions.

## Key Points

- Russian State Duma scheduled to review law targeting exiled critics on May 26
- Draft targets critics convicted of 'discrediting armed forces,' foreign agent violations, or 'disrespecting' the state
- Exiled Russians would lose access to digital government services, passport renewal, and notary services
- Bank accounts, assets, and online banking in Russia would be frozen
- Officials explicitly aim to make exiles' lives 'not better than in prison'
- Belarus already implements similar passport restrictions on overseas nationals
- Demonstrates urgent need for self-sovereign identity and censorship-resistant financial systems
