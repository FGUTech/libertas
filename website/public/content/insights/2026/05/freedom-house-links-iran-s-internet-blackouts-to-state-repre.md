# Freedom House Links Iran's Internet Blackouts to State Repression Strategy

**TL;DR:** Freedom House connects Iran's ongoing internet blackouts and digital repression to broader authoritarian strategy of engineering distrust and fracturing opposition movements.

## Key Points

- Iran conducting longest state-imposed internet blackout in history to cover accelerating executions
- Over 630 people executed in Iran in 2026 amid mass slaughter of protesters estimated in tens of thousands
- Regime using digital harassment, disinformation, and smear campaigns to fracture opposition movements
- World Values Survey identifies Iran among lowest-trust societies globally
- Freedom House launched Iran initiative focusing on restoring internet access and countering propaganda
- Authoritarian regimes survive by engineering distrust through digital tools, not just physical repression
- Historical parallel drawn to Polish Solidarity movement's success through unity over factionalism
