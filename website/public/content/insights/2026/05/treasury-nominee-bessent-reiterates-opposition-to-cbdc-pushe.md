# Treasury Nominee Bessent Reiterates Opposition to CBDC, Pushes Crypto Clarity Act

**TL;DR:** Treasury Secretary nominee Scott Bessent reaffirmed opposition to a U.S. central bank digital currency and urged Congress to pass cryptocurrency regulatory clarity legislation.

## Key Points

- Scott Bessent reiterated 'no CBDC' stance during Thursday press briefing
- Urged House and Senate to advance the Clarity Act to completion
- Signals Trump administration's continued opposition to central bank digital currency
- Clarity Act would provide regulatory framework for cryptocurrency industry
- Position aligns with concerns about CBDC enabling financial surveillance
- Congressional action needed to formalize crypto regulatory approach
