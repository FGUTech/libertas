# Mexican Journalists Jailed Under New AI Manipulation Laws After Gov Corruption Coverage

**TL;DR:** Mexican journalists arrested on charges of 'illegal use of AI' after reporting on governor corruption video that official claimed was deepfaked, highlighting weaponization of AI laws against press.

## Key Points

- Journalists Eréndira Reyes and Alejandra Hermosillo arrested May 21 in San Luis Potosí, Mexico
- Charged with 'deliberate and illegal manipulation of digital identity' under new AI-related laws
- Arrests connected to Facebook video alleging Gov. Ricardo Gallardo corruption, which he claimed was AI-manipulated
- San Luis Potosí amended penal code in 2025 to criminalize AI-manipulated images with up to 3 years prison
- Committee to Protect Journalists calls charges 'vague' and arrests appear retaliatory for critical reporting
- Arrest warrants reportedly exist for several other journalists on same charges
- Both journalists remain detained at La Pila state penitentiary according to National Registry of Detentions
- Case demonstrates how AI/deepfake laws can be weaponized to suppress journalism and dissent
