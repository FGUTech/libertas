# CPJ Warns Journalists of Border Surveillance, Device Seizures at 2026 FIFA World Cup

**TL;DR:** Committee to Protect Journalists warns 50,000 accredited World Cup journalists may face border questioning, device confiscation, and immigration enforcement targeting their reporting work.

## Key Points

- CPJ issued travel advisory for 50,000 journalists covering 2026 FIFA World Cup across US, Mexico, Canada
- Recent cases show US immigration officials detaining, denying entry, and deporting journalists for their work
- Border guards may seize devices and subject journalists to additional screening regardless of press credentials
- CPJ CEO states immigration enforcement being used as tool to silence reporting
- Organization recommends offline emergency contacts due to potential device confiscation
- Mexican journalists covering corruption/human rights face harassment, threats, physical violence
- Canadian border stops and rare arrests of journalists covering protests documented
- CPJ provides WhatsApp safety chatbot (+1-206-590-6191) for real-time operational security guidance
