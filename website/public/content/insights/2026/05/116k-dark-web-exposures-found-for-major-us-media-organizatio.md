# 116K Dark Web Exposures Found for Major US Media Organizations

**TL;DR:** Proton's research found over 116,000 dark web exposures tied to NY Times, Washington Post, and WSJ email addresses, including 12,000+ plaintext passwords, creating cybersecurity risks for journalists and sources.

## Key Points

- Proton analyzed dark web marketplaces and found 116,000+ exposures across 35,000+ email addresses from three major US media outlets
- Leaked data includes over 12,000 plaintext passwords and 61,000+ pieces of personally identifiable information
- Exposures primarily result from third-party breaches (LinkedIn, Adobe, Dropbox, etc.) rather than direct attacks on media companies
- Over 2,500 email addresses were exposed ten or more times, indicating persistent vulnerability
- Leaks create risks for targeted cyberattacks, blackmail, social engineering, and source exposure
- US ranks 64th on World Press Freedom Index, facing growing legal and political pressure on journalists
- Proton followed responsible disclosure, notifying affected publications before publication
