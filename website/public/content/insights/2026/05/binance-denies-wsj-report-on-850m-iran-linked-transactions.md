# Binance Denies WSJ Report on $850M Iran-Linked Transactions

**TL;DR:** Binance CEO Richard Teng denied Wall Street Journal allegations that $850 million in Iran-linked transactions flowed through the exchange to the IRGC.

## Key Points

- Wall Street Journal reported $850 million in Iran-linked transactions through Binance
- Transactions allegedly connected to Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC)
- Binance CEO Richard Teng publicly denied the allegations
- Highlights ongoing tensions between crypto exchanges and sanctions compliance
- Iran faces extensive financial sanctions, making crypto a potential workaround
- Unverified claims - conflicting statements between WSJ reporting and Binance denial
- Demonstrates continued scrutiny of crypto's role in sanctions circumvention
