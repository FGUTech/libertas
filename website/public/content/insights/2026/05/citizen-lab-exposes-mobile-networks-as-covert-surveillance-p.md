# Citizen Lab Exposes Mobile Networks as Covert Surveillance Platforms

**TL;DR:** New Citizen Lab research reveals how weaknesses in mobile network technology and governance enable sophisticated surveillance campaigns targeting users.

## Key Points

- Citizen Lab researchers documented surveillance campaigns exploiting mobile network vulnerabilities
- Study co-authored by Swantje Lange and Gary Miller exposes governance and technical weaknesses
- Mobile networks described as 'highly opaque and extremely complex' enabling covert surveillance
- Research shows how network infrastructure can be weaponized for espionage operations
- Findings highlight systemic privacy risks in telecommunications infrastructure
- Academic validation of mobile network surveillance threats from established research institution
