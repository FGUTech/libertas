# 116K Dark Web Exposures Found Tied to Major US News Organizations

**TL;DR:** Proton's research found over 116,000 dark web exposures linked to NYT, Washington Post, and WSJ employee emails, including 12,000+ plaintext passwords and 61,000+ pieces of PII.

## Key Points

- Proton analyzed dark web marketplaces and found 116,000+ exposures across 35,000+ email addresses from three major US news outlets
- Data includes over 12,000 plaintext passwords and 61,000+ pieces of personally identifiable information
- Exposures primarily from third-party breaches (retailers, software providers) rather than direct attacks on news organizations
- Over 2,500 email addresses exposed ten or more times, creating persistent vulnerability
- Research conducted with Constella Intelligence using responsible disclosure practices
- Leaked credentials create risks for targeted attacks, blackmail, social engineering, and source exposure
- US ranks 64th on World Press Freedom Index, facing growing legal and political pressure on journalists
