# EFF Exposes Mass ALPR Mission Creep: School Checks, Employment Screening

**TL;DR:** EFF analysis of millions of Flock Safety ALPR searches reveals police using license plate surveillance for school residency verification and employment background checks, far beyond original crime-fighting purpose.

## Key Points

- EFF analyzed millions of Flock Safety ALPR database searches by law enforcement
- Police using license plate surveillance for school residency verification, employment background checks, and noise complaints
- Buford City Schools ran 375+ searches for residency verification in 15 months, accessing 5,800+ networks nationwide
- Single searches reveal comprehensive movement patterns: medical visits, worship, travel, personal activities
- Multiple agencies across 9 states documented using ALPR for non-criminal investigations
- No warrant requirement enables unrestricted access to sensitive location data
- Demonstrates systematic mission creep from crime-fighting tool to universal movement tracker
- Agencies share data broadly in nationwide pools, amplifying surveillance reach
