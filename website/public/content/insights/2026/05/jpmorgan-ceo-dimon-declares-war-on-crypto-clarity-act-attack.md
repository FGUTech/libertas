# JPMorgan CEO Dimon Declares War on Crypto Clarity Act, Attacks Coinbase

**TL;DR:** JPMorgan's Jamie Dimon vows to fight the Clarity Act, claiming it lets crypto firms operate like banks without regulatory obligations, while calling Coinbase CEO Armstrong 'full of sh*t'.

## Key Points

- JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon publicly opposes the pending Clarity Act crypto legislation
- Claims the Act allows crypto firms to pay interest on stablecoins without banking protections
- Argues crypto platforms need full banking regulations: AML, BSA, FDIC insurance, capital requirements
- Warns stablecoin rewards will accelerate deposit flight from traditional banks
- Calls Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong 'full of sh*t' for lobbying efforts in Washington
- American Bankers Association and community banks aligned against current bill form
- Dimon frames this as existential fight for traditional banking model
