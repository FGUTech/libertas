# DRC Uses Cyber Defense Council for Secret Detention of Dissidents

**TL;DR:** Congo's National Cyber Defense Council is arbitrarily arresting activists and holding them in secret locations without charges, marking return to Kabila-era repression tactics.

## Key Points

- Human Rights Watch documented cases where National Cyber Defense Council (CNC) forcibly disappeared dissidents
- Victims held in secret detention sites without formal charges or lawyer access
- CNC operates as presidential agency outside judicial oversight
- Families allowed weekly visits while blindfolded to unknown locations
- Government claims detainees held in 'villas' pending investigation completion
- Crackdown targets journalists, activists, and political opposition members
- Officials promise CNC reforms requiring judicial police presence for arrests
- Pattern mirrors Joseph Kabila era repression methods
