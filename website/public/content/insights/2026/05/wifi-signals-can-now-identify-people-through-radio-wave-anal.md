# WiFi Signals Can Now Identify People Through Radio Wave Analysis

**TL;DR:** Researchers demonstrate WiFi sensing technology that identifies individuals by analyzing how WiFi signals interact with their bodies, creating surveillance capabilities from existing infrastructure.

## Key Points

- WiFi sensing technology uses radio wave propagation analysis to identify people in physical spaces
- Method analyzes how WiFi signals are reflected, scattered, or absorbed by human bodies
- Technology works similar to cameras but uses radio waves instead of light waves
- Research led by KIT professor Thorsten Strufe demonstrates practical implementation
- Technique leverages existing WiFi infrastructure without requiring additional hardware
- Creates new surveillance vector that operates without user knowledge or consent
- Potentially undermines location privacy and anonymity in WiFi-enabled spaces
