# Press Freedom Groups Urge New Guatemala AG to End Journalist Persecution

**TL;DR:** Nine international organizations led by CPJ call on Guatemala's new attorney general to stop criminal prosecutions of journalists, with 20+ reporters fled the country in 2022-2023 due to systematic targeting.

## Key Points

- Committee to Protect Journalists leads coalition urging new AG Gabriel García Luna to end journalist persecution
- Public Prosecutor's Office systematically targeted independent journalists for eight years through legal harassment
- Over 20 journalists fled Guatemala between 2022-2023 due to prosecution threats
- Journalist José Rubén Zamora imprisoned in widely condemned retaliation for corruption reporting
- Pattern includes forced exile, arbitrary detention, and legal harassment of media workers
- Organizations demand dismissal of unfounded cases tied to journalistic work
- Call for safe return guarantees for exiled reporters and prosecutor office independence
- Killings of media workers continue with near-total impunity
