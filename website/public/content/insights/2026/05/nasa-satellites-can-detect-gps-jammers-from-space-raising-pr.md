# NASA Satellites Can Detect GPS Jammers from Space, Raising Privacy Concerns

**TL;DR:** NASA satellites originally designed for weather monitoring can now locate GPS jammers within several kilometers, potentially enabling state surveillance of privacy tools used by activists and dissidents.

## Key Points

- NASA satellites detected GPS jammer location in Iran within several kilometers in experimental test
- Technology repurposes weather satellites designed for cyclone and ice sheet monitoring
- Sean Gorman of Zephr.xyz demonstrated capability using two different NASA satellite systems
- Cannot provide real-time monitoring or pinpoint exact locations currently
- Dual-use implications: beneficial for navigation safety but enables surveillance of privacy tools
- GPS jammers sometimes used legitimately by activists to avoid tracking
- Could help authorities identify 'high risk areas' for aircraft and maritime shipping
- Technology still experimental with significant limitations on precision and timing
