# Texas Forms Bitcoin Reserve Committee, Moves to Direct BTC Custody

**TL;DR:** Texas announced a 5-member advisory committee for its Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and issued RFP for crypto custodian to transition from ETF exposure to direct Bitcoin custody.

## Key Points

- Acting Texas Comptroller Kelly Hancock named 5-member Texas Strategic Bitcoin Reserve Advisory Committee
- Committee established under Senate Bill 21, passed by 89th Texas Legislature in June 2025
- Texas currently holds ~$10M Bitcoin exposure via iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT)
- State issued RFP seeking qualified crypto custodian for direct Bitcoin custody within 60 days
- Committee includes bitcoin mining CEO, legal scholar, institutional finance experts
- Move represents shift from ETF-based exposure to direct Bitcoin ownership and custody
- Federal Bitcoin reserve efforts continue facing legal hurdles despite Trump executive order
- American Reserves Modernization Act would authorize Treasury to buy 200k BTC annually for 5 years
